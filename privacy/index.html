Privacy Policy

Last updated July 16, 2026.

Your privacy is important to us. It is BadgeDrops Team's policy to respect your privacy and comply with any applicable law and regulation regarding any personal information we may collect about you, including across our website, https://badgedrops.com.

badgedrops.com is a free, informational website that displays publicly available Twitch badge and emote data. It does not require an account, and we do not sell products, run newsletters, or knowingly collect personal information from our visitors. This policy explains the limited data that is handled when you use the site.

What We Collect

We do not ask you for personal information, and the site has no sign-up, login, comment, or contact form. The only information involved in your visit is the standard technical data described below, which is handled automatically by the services that host and deliver the site.

Log Data

Like almost every website, when you visit badgedrops.com the servers that deliver it may automatically log standard technical data provided by your web browser. This may include your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, your browser type and version, the pages you visit, and the time and date of your visit. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and this log data is collected and processed by GitHub in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not maintain our own analytics of this data.

Cookies and Tracking

badgedrops.com does not set tracking cookies, does not use advertising networks, and does not run third-party analytics or tracking scripts. Your browser may store standard technical data (such as cached files) as it does for any website, but we do not use this to identify or profile you.

Third-Party Services and Links

To operate, badgedrops.com relies on a small number of third parties:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves the website;
  • Twitch — the source of the publicly available badge and emote data shown on the site; and
  • Google Fonts — used to load the site's typeface.

Our website also links to external sites, such as Twitch channels and categories. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we have no control over their content or practices. After following a link to any third-party content, you should read that site's posted privacy policy. This Privacy Policy does not apply to any of your activities after you leave badgedrops.com.

Security of Your Information

We protect the site within commercially acceptable means to prevent loss, theft, and unauthorised access. Although we and our hosting providers do our best to protect the site, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

Children's Privacy

We do not aim any of our content directly at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information about children under 13.

If we decide to change this privacy policy, we will post the changes here at the same link by which you are accessing this privacy policy. Any changes will take effect immediately from the date of publication.
